You are an expert web developer. Create a fully functional Invoice Generator web app using React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.

The app must include:
- A form to fill in: sender info (name, address, email), client info (name, address, email), invoice number, invoice date, and due date
- A dynamic line items table where users can add/remove rows with: description, quantity, unit price, and auto-calculated total per row
- Auto-calculation of subtotal, tax (configurable percentage), and grand total
- A clean, professional invoice preview that updates in real-time as the user types
- A "Download as PDF" button that exports the invoice preview
- A "Clear / New Invoice" button to reset the form
- Responsive design that works on desktop and mobile
- Professional color scheme suitable for business use

Make the UI clean, modern, and print-friendly.
